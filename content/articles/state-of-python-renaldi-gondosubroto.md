For those new to the language or looking for a refresher, we'll delve into how
the Python community currently is in Melbourne and discuss its growing
significance in the tech world. The latter half of the session will then cover
usage of GenAI in Python and show a use case in a popular newcomer in the GenAI
space, Amazon Bedrock.
