## Ben Taylor, Product Engineering Team Lead, Stile Education

<https://www.linkedin.com/in/taybenlor/>

Ben, a software engineer passionate about EdTech, will share his experience of adapting the Runno sandbox, originally written in TypeScript for browser environments, into a Python package. Over the holidays, Ben released this tool to PyPI, enabling developers to safely run untrusted code from various languages directly within Python. This talk will cover the challenges of bridging languages, the importance of sandboxing and AI workflows, and how this tool can enhance projects involving LLMs and interactive learning environments.
