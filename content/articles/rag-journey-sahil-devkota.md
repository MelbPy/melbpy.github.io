## Sahil Devkota, Full Stack Developer

<https://www.linkedin.com/in/sahil-bahadur-devkota-b874a8306/>

Sahil starts where a lot of us have: an AI app that kept hallucinating. He'll
trace the path from plain RAG through fine-tuning experiments across different
models, and explain why he landed back in the RAG world — just a far more
agentic version of it. Expect a walkthrough of the actual Jupyter notebook, the
retrieval logic, the results, and a clear sense of when to reach for which
approach.
