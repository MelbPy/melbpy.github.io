## Luca Lenz, Intern, Legal Tech Helper

<https://www.linkedin.com/in/lucalenz/>

Luca, a mathematics and scientific computing graduate from Heidelberg
University, will share insights from his recent work at Legal Tech Helper. His
talk explores Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG) and how it enhances AI
chatbot reliability, particularly in sensitive applications like legal and
medical services. He will discuss common challenges of LLMs, and introduce
tools like Docling, Milvus, and Ollama for implementing RAG with self-hosted
LLMs.
