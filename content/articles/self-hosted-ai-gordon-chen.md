## Gordon Chen, Managing Director, GordonData

<https://www.linkedin.com/in/gordonjhchen/>

Tired of generic AI answers or high cloud costs? This talk dives deep into how
we solved the enterprise knowledge challenge by building a secure, self-hosted
RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) system. It details the specific
open-source stack, from SharePoint ingestion and FAISS indexing to running
DeepSeek-LLM locally, all integrated directly into Microsoft Teams.
