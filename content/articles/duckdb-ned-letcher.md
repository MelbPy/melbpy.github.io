## Ned Letcher, Lead Data Science Engineer, Thoughtworks

<https://www.linkedin.com/in/nletcher/>

In this session, DuckDB is showcased as a highly efficient in-process analytical database that is capturing the attention of professionals across the data spectrum, owing to its ease of use, exceptional performance, and comprehensive feature set. The talk delves into DuckDB's essence by outlining its capabilities, comparing it with other analytical databases, and providing hands-on examples to illustrate its application in various scenarios. These include ad-hoc data analysis, serverless data pipelines, analytical applications in web browsers, data lake querying for machine learning, and as a compact data warehouse for business intelligence. By the end of the session, attendees are expected to have a clear understanding of DuckDB's potential applications and receive practical tips on how to start integrating it into their own projects, highlighting its versatility and effectiveness in handling diverse analytical tasks.
