## Aman Madaan, Data Science/GenAI Engineer, ANZ Bank

<https://www.linkedin.com/in/aman-madaan/>

Prompt ops is emerging as a critical discipline in the era of LLMs. In this
talk, I will explore how Prompt Ops enables scalable, reliable and maintainable
prompt engineering workflows. We will dive into best practices for versioning,
testing, evaluating and deploying prompts - turning prompt engineering from an
art into a science. Whether you are building internal copilots, customer facing
chatbots or LLM powered data tools, this session will equip you with tools and
mindset to operationalize your prompts like code.
