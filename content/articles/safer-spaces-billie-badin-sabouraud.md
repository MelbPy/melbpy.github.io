## Billie Badin-Sabouraud, Engineering Manager, Thales Australia

<https://www.linkedin.com/in/billiebadin/>

Billie introduces a work-in-progress project leveraging Python APIs to monitor,
track, and document social media hate speech across platforms like LinkedIn,
Instagram, Threads, and BlueSky. Attendees will gain insights into scripting
practical solutions for detecting and countering online abuse and may join an
open-source collaborative effort to foster safer digital environments for all.
